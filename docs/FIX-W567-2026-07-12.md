## AX product decisions (locked 2026-07-12)

Three genuine product/cost calls were made by AX before implementation:

- **W5 digests → opt OUT of clustering entirely.** 群聊日报 / AI HOT are
  multi-topic curation, not single-event coverage. They still render as
  standalone feed cards but never join or bridge event clusters.
- **W6a tombstone re-open → on cohesion breach.** Null `verified_at` when some
  member of a verified cluster has drifted more than **0.38** from the cluster
  **lead** (the same lead-anchored cohesion metric the Stage-A join gate uses,
  with a 0.03 hysteresis band above the 0.35 gate) — lowest LLM cost, targets
  the incoherent fusions specifically (not routine growth).
- **W6b commentary regen → growth 2× OR tier upgrade.** Regenerate when
  `member_count ≥ 2× the count at last commentary`, OR when `event_tier`
  crosses into featured/p1 and the full analysis was never written.

## Loop-safety invariants (why none of these thrash)

- **W6a**: candidate predicate requires `latest_member_at > verified_at`. On
  re-verify (arbitration keeps it), `verified_at` moves to now with no new
  member → not eligible again until a new member actually joins. No re-verify
  loop.
- **W6b**: after regen, `commentary_member_count = member_count` (won't fire
  until it doubles again) and full-tier regen sets `editor_analysis_zh`
  (tier-upgrade branch won't re-fire). No regen loop.
- **W5.1 / W4b**: pure join-time gates — they only ever *reject* a join
  (fall through to unclustered/singleton), never create work.
